Security Architecture

Six layers of cryptographic and risk isolation.

  1. 1

    Tier-1 Security Audits & On-Chain Timelocks

    The Rust matching engine, threshold cryptography, and clearing vaults undergo independent tier-1 audits prior to mainnet. Core risk parameter updates are governed by a multi-sig Security Council and mandatory on-chain timelocks.

  2. 2

    Threshold IBTE (BLS12-381) & Cryptographic Slashing

    Order payloads are sealed client-side and unlocked in memory by a 5-of-7 node quorum only after 50 ms batch sequencing is committed, enforced by cryptographic fraud proofs and validator bond slashing.

  3. 3

    Multi-Source Oracle Guards & Price Collars

    A composite price feed across institutional oracles and top spot venues filters out manipulation spikes in real time, while automated price collars transition extreme volatility into a protective Call Auction.

  4. 4

    Tiered Partial Liquidations & Zero-Sum Clearing

    Every long contract is matched peer-to-peer against a funded short in native USDC. Margin breaches trigger a grace window followed by incremental partial liquidations—preserving trader equity against flash wicks.

  5. 5

    SAFU Insurance Waterfall & Reserve Backing

    A dedicated SAFU reserve—capitalized at launch and continuously replenished by protocol fee revenue—absorbs extreme market gap slippage and backs instant cross-chain Soft-Credit.

  6. 6

    Non-Custodial Vaults & Scoped Permissions

    Collateral remains in auditable on-chain clearing contracts. Ephemeral session keys and copy-trading managers can execute trades within strict risk bounds, but hold zero withdrawal privileges.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How do encrypted micro-batches reduce adverse selection and MEV?

On transparent CLOBs, latency bots snipe stale market maker quotes during external price moves and sandwich large directional orders (≥$50k) in the mempool—forcing makers to widen spreads. Dywe encrypts order parameters client-side via BLS12-381 threshold encryption (IBTE) inside 50 ms micro-batches, decrypts in matching-engine memory (~35 ms), and executes New (ALO) → Cancel → Taker priority before fills clear. In our open-source Rust backtest across Native L1 Binance Futures data, this cuts 1s toxic MM markout by 40.4% vs. FIFO (−87.1% stale sniping) with 0.0% dropped volume and only +0.005 bps overhead at zero MEV, while cutting taker All-In costs by 22%–48% when MEV is present.

Who holds custody of my collateral?

You always maintain sovereign control. All native USDC deposits reside in audited, non-custodial smart contracts. Neither the exchange sequencer, nor copy-trading vault operators, nor any automated system has withdrawal permissions over your principal.

Do I have to sign a wallet popup for every single order?

No. Dywe uses scoped ephemeral session keys (EIP-4337). You sign once when opening a trading session, granting the client terminal local, temporary permission strictly to place and cancel orders in the order book. Session keys hold zero withdrawal privileges, giving you seamless 1-click execution at CEX speed without sacrificing non-custodial security.

How does Universal Multi-Chain Soft-Credit (<2s) work?

Traditional cross-chain transfers can take minutes to finalize. With Dywe, as soon as your native Circle CCTP deposit burn is verified on the source network, the protocol instantly credits a Trade-Only balance backed by dedicated liquidity reserves. You can start trading on 10–20 liquid pairs in under 2 seconds, while background canonical attestation finalizes.

Which networks can I deposit from?

At launch, native USDC deposits clear directly across Arbitrum, Base, Solana, Ethereum, and BNB Chain (BSC) via official Circle CCTP burn-and-mint mechanics. Our modular adapter architecture is designed to expand across 20+ additional chains without wrapped tokens or synthetic bridges.

What is the fee schedule?

Dywe operates on a transparent exchange fee tier: standard taker fees are just 0.020% (2 bps), while qualifying institutional market makers receive a negative maker rebate of −0.005% under dedicated SLAs. There are zero hidden borrow fees or synthetic swap penalties.

How do liquidations work, and am I protected from flash wicks?

The protocol enforces multi-layered risk guards: a median oracle filter rejects outlier price manipulation spikes in real time. If margin thresholds are breached, positions enter a 15-second grace period followed by partial liquidations in 25% tranches—protecting the remaining 75% of your position rather than wiping out your entire equity at once. Extreme market gap risks are further absorbed by the protocol's dedicated SAFU insurance fund.

How does non-custodial copy-trading work?

Experienced quant desks and verified traders deploy autonomous ERC-4626 smart vaults. Followers deposit native USDC directly into the vault smart contract. Vault leaders receive a restricted trading-only role (max 10x leverage, hard drawdown circuit-breakers) and can never withdraw follower assets. Leader orders execute atomically within sealed IBTE batches, guaranteeing followers are never front-run.

How do developer integrations and the Builder SDK work?

Developers building non-custodial wallets, custom trading interfaces, or algorithmic execution bots can connect via our zero-bond Volume-Gated SDK. Higher API rate limits and low-latency WebSocket quotas unlock automatically as your volume grows, while verified ecosystem partners can apply for custom partner revenue tiers and grant programs.
